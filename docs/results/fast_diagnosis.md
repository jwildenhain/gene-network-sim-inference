# Why FAST ranked low

Generated by `scripts/diagnose_fast.py --render` from `docs/results/fast_diagnosis.json`; `tests/test_fast_identity.py` fails when the page and the data differ.

## The cause

FAST estimates `A_0 = (x - u) X^+`. For a full-rank X, `X X^+ = I`, so `A_0 = I + A`: the diagonal of every row is about 1 and is the largest entry of the row. Step 4 keeps the K largest entries per row and refits them, so one of the K slots of every row goes to a self-edge that is not in the network (the simulated ground truth has no self-regulation, and the scorer counts a predicted self-edge as a false positive). With K at the network's mean in-degree (2 to 3) that removes a third to a half of the regulators FAST could find. The rank-(N-1) truncation also leaves `I - X X^+` noise on the diagonal, so subtracting the identity alone is not enough: the corrected ranking uses `|A_0 - I|` and never selects the diagonal.

The identity is right for the E. coli SOS data (fast.m adds it back when N = 9), which is why the MATLAB code has it; `FastSVD(rank_without_identity=False)` is that MATLAB-identical selection and stays the class default (the equivalence tests pin it). The registry, hence the studio, the job queue, the archive and the sweeps, uses `rank_without_identity=True`; where the diagonal correction is already on (SOS) the option changes nothing.

## Reading

- On Hill data (the studio's default) dropping the identity lifts FAST from F1 0.35 to 0.45 at 50 genes and from 0.28 to 0.35 at 100, to the level of NIR (0.45 and 0.37) and above it in AUPR at 50 genes. The adaptive per-row model order (BIC) adds a little on top.
- On the linear engine the gain is real but small in absolute terms (0.12 to 0.16 at 50 genes, 0.03 to 0.05 at 100) and FAST stays well below NIR: the remaining gap is the unregularised truncated-SVD inverse of a noisy, ill-conditioned X. Larger SVD damping (`svd_reg` 1e-3 to 3e-1) was tried and did not help on Hill data (F1 0.43 down to 0.34-0.36) and moved the linear case by less than the seed spread, so it was left at 1e-6.
- The archive's FAST rows and the leaderboards were recomputed with the corrected ranking (see CHANGELOG 1.0.5); numbers quoted from earlier releases for FAST are from the MATLAB ranking.
