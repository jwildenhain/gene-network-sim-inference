Generated by `scripts/run_vcc_benchmark.py --report` from the stored simulations (1000 simulations, 100 settings); every number below is computed from them and `tests/test_vcc_findings.py` fails if this file and the data disagree. Score: direct-edge AUPR over the perturbed genes. Comparisons are matched (one factor changed, simulations paired by data generator and seed) with two-sided paired Wilcoxon p-values (across data generators: paired by seed, the same graph for every generator).

## Which algorithms work

* Beat both the random ranking and the hubness-only control over the whole corpus: 20 of 20 (sfinn, cellbox, s2g, mean_difference, ridge_cv, causalgrn, nir, fast, inspre, genie3, deeprig, score_vote, arachne, pearson, g2s, notears, netdeconv, vote, grnboost, dmdgrn).
* Only at the hubness level (marginal): none.
* Not better than random: none.
* Dropped before the benchmark (they fail the admission test on noise-free data, tests/test_vcc_algorithm_admission.py): cpa_lite: fails the admission test: no better than the hubness-only control even on noise-free data; grnboost_profiles: GRNBoost on per-perturbation effect profiles is at the hubness level; the cell-level variant is used.
* Rank 1: sfinn (AUPR 0.384, 95% CI 0.376-0.390, rank interval 1-1); separable from all others.
