# Held-out perturbation prediction on simulated screens

Generated by `scripts/heldout_screen_benchmark.py` (method and metrics: `grn_sim.evaluation.heldout_screen`). Each simulated screen perturbs every gene once; 5-fold cross-validation over the perturbed genes; the predictors see responses estimated from the simulated cells and are scored against the noise-free total effects, on off-target genes only. Mean ± standard deviation over 5 seeds; medium noise, CRISPRi knockdown, 40 cells per perturbation.

Metrics: `pearson_delta` correlation of predicted and true response; `deg_mse_skill` 1 - MSE/MSE(no effect) on the genes the perturbation truly moved (above 0 beats predicting no effect); `direction` share with the right sign; `top_overlap` share of the truly affected genes among the k largest predicted effects. `silent` perturbations move no off-target gene; only `active` ones (at least one moved gene) have all four metrics.
