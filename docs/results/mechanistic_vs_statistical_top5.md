# Inference on mechanistic and on statistical data: the top 5 algorithms

Generated by `scripts/render_top5_results.py` from `src/grn_sim/data/benchmarks/vcc_screen_benchmark.jsonl` (the Virtual Cell screen corpus behind `vcc_screen_benchmark.md`); a test fails if this page and the data differ. Nothing here is typed in by hand except the wording.

## What is compared

- **Corpus:** 22,000 rows, one per simulated screen and algorithm; 1,000 screens, 200 per generator.
- **Mechanistic:** the Hill-kinetics engine of the studio (200 screens).
- **Statistical:** four statistical generators pooled (800 screens): linear SEM, nonlinear additive-noise SCM, negative-binomial copula, probabilistic Boolean network.
- **Settings (all generators together):** perturbation mode input 50, knockdown 800, knockout 150; genes 50 (50), 100 (850), 200 (100); cells per perturbation 40. Noise level, size, perturbation type and pathway architecture are pooled.
- **Measure:** AUPR against the direct edges (`aupr_direct`). *lift* = mean AUPR / mean chance AUPR; *P@k* = precision at the true number of edges; *AUPR total* = AUPR against total (direct plus indirect) effects; *median s* = median run time of the algorithm on one screen. The ± is the SD across screens; the 95% CI is 1.96 SD/√n for the mean (a normal approximation, not a paired test).
- **Ranked:** the 20 algorithms; the controls (`random`, `gene_features`) are shown as reference levels, not ranked.

## Comparison

- Best mean AUPR: mechanistic cellbox 0.499, statistical sfinn 0.336. Spread across screens (SD of the best five): mechanistic 0.122–0.230, statistical 0.051–0.093.
- Rank agreement over the 20 algorithms: Spearman ρ = 0.85. The two top 5 share cellbox, ridge_cv, causalgrn.
- Largest rank changes (mechanistic → statistical): mean_difference 8 → 2; notears 12 → 17; score_vote 15 → 10; sfinn 6 → 1; arachne 16 → 12.

## Limits of this page

- The corpus is simulated Perturb-seq screens, not the 500-2000 gene studio runs of `SCALE_BENCHMARK.md`, and most of its screens use knockdown perturbations, where the perturbed gene's own response is visible. Algorithms that need that (CellBox) rank high here and do not on the regulator-input data of the scale runs.
- It predates the algorithm fixes of 2026-11-12 (inspre in regulator mode, X + U for the co-expression methods, K per target): those matter mainly for regulator-input data, so a re-run of the corpus would show their effect. It was not re-run.
- Screens differ in noise, size, perturbation type and architecture, which are pooled here; the per-factor tables are in `vcc_screen_benchmark.md`. The CIs treat screens as independent, which they are not entirely (10 seeds per setting).
